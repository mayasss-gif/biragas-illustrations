The platform

One engine, 25 modules, one question

Most tools in biology report what moves together. BiRAGAS is built to say what causes what, to score how far that claim can be trusted, and to refuse to answer when the evidence will not carry one. The modules below are not separate products; they are the parts of a single engine, and the agent interface sits in front of all of them.

IngestRead the data, check it, and put every dataset on a common footing.
ReasonWork out what causes what, and score how much the claim can be trusted.
DeliverReturn a source-linked, regulator-shaped answer, not a ranked list.

Ask in plain language

Ask Causion routes a question to the modules it needs, configures them and returns a result. No pipeline engineer in the loop.

Source-linked outputs

Every output carries the evidence it rests on, which is what turns an answer into something a reviewer can check.

Built to be refused

Where the evidence does not support a causal claim the engine says so. A platform that always answers is not measuring anything.

Where it goes next

The engineering roadmap, in 7 phases

Taken from the published graph neural network roadmap. The order matters: each phase is the ground the next one stands on, and the causal phase is deliberately after the retrieval and reasoning phases rather than before them.

1. FoundationsData, graphs and the engineering rails everything else runs on.
2. KG Embeddings & RetrievalA pathway-aware retrieval backbone over the knowledge graph.
3. GNNs for ReasoningNeuro-symbolic reasoning across pathways and the knowledge graph.
4. Causal DiscoveryThe move from observational association to interventional claim.
5. Multi-Disease GNNsShared mechanisms across diseases, and the repurposing that follows.
6. Patient-level / Temporal GNNsCohort graphs tied to clinical outcomes over time.
7. Productionization & GovernanceDeployment that is safe, fast and auditable.
