Page seven

And the order they run in

Step 1
Data Ingestion & Validation
Unpack the shopping and check nothing is off before you cook.
Step 2
Temporal Causality Analysis
Work out what happened first, because causes do not arrive late.
Step 3
Network Inference
Draw the map of who is connected to whom, with arrows, not just lines.
Step 4
Perturbation Analysis
Push the thing and watch what moves. The closest you get to an experiment.
Step 5
Evidence Integration
Put the four accounts side by side and see where they agree.
Step 6
Statistical Validation
Try to break your own finding before anyone else does.
